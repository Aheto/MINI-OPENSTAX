📘 Understanding Number Sets

In mathematics, we group numbers to make counting, measuring, and calculating easier. Let's explore the three fundamental sets used in everyday life.

🔍 Natural Numbers (N)

These are the counting numbers. We use them when we count objects: apples, students, or goals scored in a match.

Set: {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, ...}

🔢 Whole Numbers (W)

Whole numbers include all natural numbers plus zero. Zero represents "none" or the starting point.

Set: {0, 1, 2, 3, 4, ...}

🌡️ Integers (Z)

Integers expand whole numbers to include negative numbers. These are essential for representing temperatures below freezing, money owed (debt), or depth below sea level.

Set: {..., -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ...}
